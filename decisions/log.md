*Every real decision, with the reasoning behind it. Claude adds an entry whenever you make a call worth remembering.*

Related: [[references/how-i-decide]] (the thinking behind these entries), [[memory/taste]] (preferences, which are not decisions), [[inbox]] (where a decision often shows up first)

## Why this file exists

Six months from now you will remember what you decided and forget why. Without the why, a good decision looks arbitrary, and arbitrary decisions get quietly undone.

So each entry keeps the reasoning next to the choice: what you picked, what you knew at the time, what else was on the table, and what would make you look at it again. That also ends the loop of re-arguing the same question every few months because nobody wrote down how it was settled.

## Entry format

```text
## YYYY-MM-DD Short title of the decision

**Decision:** What was decided, in one or two sentences.

**Why:** The reasoning. What was true at the time that made this the right call.

**Alternatives considered:** What else was on the table and why it lost.

**Revisit when:** The condition that would make this worth reopening. A date, a number, or an event.
```

New entries go at the bottom of the log. Old entries are never edited or deleted.

If a decision is reversed, leave the old entry alone. Add a new one that links to it and gives the reversal its own reason. How your thinking changed is part of the record.
