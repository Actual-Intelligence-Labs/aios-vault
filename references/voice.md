*Your written voice. `/onboard` fills this during setup, from writing you paste in. Edit it by hand any time.*

This file exists so that anything written on your behalf sounds like you wrote it. Emails, posts, proposals, captions.

The AI reads this before it writes anything. Automatically, because of a hook, so there is no command for you to remember.

Written voice only. How you talk out loud is a different thing and lives in [[references/spoken-voice]]. Never blend the two.

## Samples (pasted verbatim, never edited)

```
{{PASTE_YOUR_REAL_WRITING_HERE}}
```

Samples must be writing you actually sent. Copy and paste them from your sent folder, your posts, your docs.

Do not compose a sample fresh in a chat window. When people write "an example of how I write" on the spot, they write more carefully than they normally do, and the AI learns a version of you that does not exist. Two or three real pieces beat ten polished ones.

## Hard rules

- Never send anything in this voice to anyone outside without showing the owner the draft first. Client emails, public posts, anything with their name on it. Draft it, show it, wait.
- Match the register above, do not improve on it. If the samples are blunt, stay blunt.
- If the samples section is still a placeholder, say so and ask for real writing rather than guessing at a voice.
- The same goes for every `{{PLACEHOLDER}}` in the Register list above. A blank there means that habit was never observed. Do not fill it from a hunch, from the samples' subject matter, or from what the business sounds like it should be. Write from the samples themselves and leave the rest blank.
