# How I decide

*Your rules of thumb for judgment calls, in your own words. It starts nearly empty on purpose. It fills in as you make real decisions, and you can edit or delete any line.*

The AI reads this when a choice needs your judgment: whether to take something on, what to say no to, how to weigh one option against another. The aim is simple. When you have explained the same kind of call twice, you should never have to explain it a third time.

## How this file grows

- **A one-off decision** goes in [[decisions/log]]: what was chosen, when, and why.
- **A rule** is what you get when the same reasoning shows up a second time. When the AI notices that, it adds one line here, under the right heading, in your words, and tells you in one line. You can say "that's not a rule" and it comes out.
- **Preferences about how work looks or sounds** belong in [[memory/taste]], not here. This file is about choices.
- **Lines carry the date they were added and where they came from**, so you can tell a rule you have tested for a year from one you stated last week.

The format, one line per rule:

`- YYYY-MM-DD the rule -> why it holds (from: the decision or note it came from)`

## When there is no rule for it yet

If a call comes up that nothing above covers, the AI helps like this:

1. Checks [[decisions/log]] for a past decision on something similar, and says what it was and why.
2. Lays out the real options, usually two or three, each with what it costs, what could go wrong, and whether it can be undone.
3. Gives a recommendation and the one reason behind it, or just the options, depending on what [[context/about-me]] says you prefer.
4. Once you decide, logs it in [[decisions/log]]. If your reasoning matches an earlier decision, it proposes a rule for this file.
