*How you sound out loud. Used only for words that will be spoken: video scripts, voiceover, podcast talking points, a pitch you will deliver live.*

## Read this first

Your spoken voice and your written voice are different, and they must never be blended.

People write in tighter sentences than they speak. They speak with more repetition, more asides, more warmth, and more filler that actually helps a listener follow along. A script written in someone's written voice sounds stiff when read aloud. A post written in their spoken voice reads as rambling.

So:

- Output that will be **read** uses [[references/voice]].
- Output that will be **spoken** uses this file.
- Never mix them in the same piece.
