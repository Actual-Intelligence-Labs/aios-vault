# Setup answers

Your answers to the setup conversation, kept in one place. Setup reads this file first, so anything already written here is something it will not ask you again.

Two ways to fill it:

- **Just talk.** Say "set me up" in Claude Code and answer one question at a time. Each answer lands here as you give it, so if you stop halfway, the next session picks up where you left off.
- **Or write here first.** Replace any "_No answer yet._" line with your answer, then say "set me up". Rough notes and fragments are fine.

Setup asks only what it still needs and shapes each question around what you have already said. Most people are done in about ten questions. It never asks more than 15.

Never put a password, a key, an account number or card details in this file or in the chat. Setup never needs one.

## Writing samples

One or two things you actually wrote and sent: an email, a post, a message to a client. Paste them exactly as they went out, typos and all, and do not write anything new for this. Text written for a setup chat sounds like the chat, not like you, and every draft made from it later would come out slightly off. Two different kinds (say, an email and a post) teach more than two of the same.

Got a recording of yourself talking, like a video, a podcast or a call you led? Its transcript is welcome too. It is kept apart from your writing, because people talk differently than they write.
